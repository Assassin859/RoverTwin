A live digital twin of a Moon rover. It stays in sync with the telemetry stream, lets you break the rover on purpose, shows how one fault spreads through power, heat, navigation and radio, predicts what happens next and simulates the way out.

  • MIRRORThe twin runs the rover's physics and corrects itself with every telemetry frame.
  • BREAKInject battery, thermal, sensor or communication faults, alone or together.
  • PREDICTIt spots what the model can't explain, finds the root cause and projects 2 hours ahead.
  • RECOVEREvery recovery option is simulated and ranked before a command is sent.
